Wealth Portal

We don't just manage wealth. We built the platform that does it.

Since our inception in 1993, and our rebrand to Family Capital in 2017, we've blended traditional values with a digital-first approach to serve the complex needs of Ultra-High Net Worth individuals and entrepreneurs. Our ethos goes beyond asset management — we focus on creating meaningful legacies through a modern lens, ensuring wealth is preserved and enhanced for generations to come.

Who We Work With

Wealthy Families

Our commitment to working with wealthy families stems from understanding the critical role of strategic wealth management in securing intergenerational prosperity. Specialising in trusts, Family Investment Companies, and other bespoke wealth preservation methods, we tailor our services to uphold not only the immediate financial well-being of our clients but also to establish a foundation for enduring wealth.

Entrepreneurs

Our partnership with entrepreneurs is built on the understanding that effective financial planning is crucial for wealth accumulation. We stress the importance of intertwining personal financial strategies with business ventures from the start. Leveraging tax efficiencies and creating safeguarding structures like trusts and Family Investment Companies are central to our methodology.

Our team at Family Capital is here to guide you through integrating your personal and business financial strategies for lasting legacy and wealth. If you're ready to take the next step, we would love to hear from you.

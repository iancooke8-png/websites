Our Timeline

Three decades of innovation, growth, and excellence

2024
September 2024
Family Capital Partnership

We're proud to announce the launch of the Family Capital Partnership — a selective route for advisers and firms who meet the highest professional standards. Partner Firms become part of the Family Capital brand, sharing our culture, infrastructure, and a commitment to putting clients first.

We're delighted to welcome Claire Stones as our first Partner Firm. Having built a highly successful business of her own, Claire now brings her team to lead Family Capital North in Leeds.

January 2024
Introducing the 'Industry Peers' Programme

We're thrilled to introduce the 'Industry Peers' Programme — a comprehensive service for planners, fund managers, and advisers. The standout feature is exclusive access to our advanced systems and the Family Wealth Portal.

2021
April 2021
Family Wealth Portal Launched

As we emerged from the COVID-19 lockdown, we seized the opportunity to reinvigorate our passion for technology. We embarked on the development of our flagship product, 'Wealth Portal', alongside mobile applications for Apple and Google Play.

March 2021
Perpetual Sells Final Stake to Schroders

Five years on from Schroder's initial acquisition, we bid farewell to our final portion of the stake. Family Capital, operating independently, remains steadfast in upholding the highest standards.

2020
November 2020
Group Rebrand to Perpetual

We rebranded the group as 'Perpetual', reflecting our commitment to longevity. Our emblem, a digital leaf, represents the fusion of nature with technology. The group comprised Family Capital and Finovate, our fintech joint initiative.

2017
March 2017
Rebrand to Family Capital

24 years after establishing Ian Cooke & Partners, we rebranded as Family Capital — redefining our approach around the family office ethos, powered by a fully digital framework.

The rebrand reflected our focus on UHNW clients and their families, recognising the complexity of their trusts, companies, and private investments.

January 2017
Schroders Buys Stake in Benchmark

Schroders, a FTSE 100 company established in the UK since the 1800s, resonated with our culture and long-term vision. Ian Cooke maintained a 51% majority, ensuring control during the transition.

2016
July 2016
Engagement of PWC

With nearly 300 staff, we reached a pivotal crossroads. Ian Cooke pointed us towards the trade route — targeting high-end asset management firms who valued compliance and sustained progress. We evaluated over 20 possibilities.

2015
September 2015
SEI Contract Renewed

We reaffirmed our partnership with SEI, swayed by their outstanding reputation in providing safe custody for client assets.

January 2015
Benchmark Capital Established

We amalgamated our enterprises into 'Benchmark Capital', symbolising the high standards each business had established, setting a 'benchmark' for others.

2015
A Remarkable Year for Awards

Five awards in one year. Aspect8 won 'Adviser Firm of the Year' at both the Money Marketing and Professional Adviser Awards — akin to winning both a BAFTA and an Oscar.

2014
March 2014
Investment Manager of the Year

Winning 'Investment Manager of the Year' validated our belief in a simpler, quantitative approach to fund management — proving effective over the long term.

2012
December 2012
Creative Technologies: 20th Firm

Our technology firm, the unsung hero with innovative IP and a talented team, onboarded its 20th firm — including pioneering tools for Court of Protection cases.

October 2012
Best Practice Achieves Chartered Status

One of the first major national firms to achieve Chartered Status. Our quarterly CPD meetings at South Lodge in Horsham were legendary — members travelled from Scotland and Ireland.

2011
March 2011
Our First Award

Winning 'Best Network of the Year' at the Money Marketing Awards — regarded as the pinnacle of industry recognition. A defining moment for our team.

January 2011
SEI Partnership Initiated

We chose SEI as our custodian partner for Fusion Wealth — a large global custodian providing rock-solid institutional security for client assets.

2010
August 2010
Aspect8 Established

We rebranded our financial planning business to 'Aspect8' — a financial octopus with eight essential arms, each representing a crucial aspect of a financial plan.

June 2010
Best Practice: 50th Member

Our compliance network welcomed its 50th wealth management firm — with zero member attrition since inception.

2008
September 2008
Sussex House

Rapid expansion as the leading network for Chartered financial planners. We acquired Sussex House in Horsham — our headquarters for the next decade.

2006
March 2006
First API Links Established

One of the earliest real-world integrations with a custody platform. We launched an 'integration fest', connecting with nearly all major retail platforms in the UK.

2003
August 2003
Best Practice IFA Group

A decade after inception, we launched an independent network — growing to over 28 financial planners specialising in holistic planning for families, trusts, and companies.

2000
May 2000
Old Bank Chambers

We moved to Hurstpierpoint, taking over the Old Bank Building formerly owned by Barclays. Ian Cooke and Partners became the UK's largest network firm of Certified Financial Planners.

1993
August 1993
Ian Cooke and Partners Established

Ian Cooke left Standard Life to establish his own independent financial advisory firm. Coincidentally, 1993 was also the year the World Wide Web became publicly accessible.

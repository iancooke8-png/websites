Our framework

Eight aspects. In order.

This isn't a menu — it's a sequence. Each step only makes sense once the one before it is sorted. But they also overlap and interact — you're always considering all eight, even when you're focused on one. Most firms skip straight to step six. We start at one.

Clear the deck
1

Mortgages

Why invest at 5% while paying 6% on debt?
▾

If someone's paying 6% on a mortgage and you invest their spare cash at 5%, you're going backwards. Paying down the mortgage is a guaranteed, tax-free return. And there's something else — when the mortgage goes, something changes. People make better decisions. They sleep differently. They take smarter risks.

2

Income Protection

Your income funds everything else on this list.
▾

Your ability to earn is your most valuable asset. If it stops, everything else stops with it — mortgage payments, pension contributions, savings. This is the foundation that makes the rest possible.

Connects to:
3

Protection

One event can unwind decades of planning.
▾

Life cover, critical illness, key person insurance. Not exciting. But without it, a single event can destroy decades of careful work. And we always put policies in trust — every single time. Why wouldn't you? Without the trust, the proceeds go through probate and potentially into the estate for IHT. With a trust, they go where you want them, when you want them.

Build the structure
4

Pensions

The most tax-efficient wrapper — if used properly.
▾

Pensions offer unmatched tax relief — but only if structured alongside everything else. Contribution strategy, drawdown planning, death benefits. Get this wrong and you leave money on the table or hit a tax charge you didn't see coming.

Connects to:
5

Savings

Liquidity gives you options.
▾

ISAs, cash reserves, emergency funds. The boring stuff that gives you freedom. How much in cash versus invested depends on your whole plan — not a rule of thumb from a weekend supplement.

Connects to:
6

Investments

Step six. Not step one.
▾

This is where most firms start. We think it should come last — after you know the tax position, the wrapper, the timescale, and the purpose. The portfolio might look balanced overall, but where each asset sits matters enormously. Growth assets in the GIA where you're not crystallising income. Dividend-heavy holdings inside the ISA where income is tax-free. Lower-risk income producers in the pension. Same allocation, completely different tax outcome.

Our DFM runs at 0.1% because the real value is in everything above it.

Connects to:
Keep it together
7

Taxation

Tax touches every single aspect.
▾

Income tax on earnings, CGT on gains, IHT on death, VAT on advice fees. Tax isn't a separate service — it runs through every decision above. If your adviser doesn't understand your full tax position, they're advising blind.

Connects to:
8

Trusts

Move wealth between generations — properly.
▾

Trusts, family investment companies, discounted gift trusts, absolute trusts. These are the structures that preserve wealth across generations — but only when integrated with everything above. Get the order wrong and you create tax charges, not savings.

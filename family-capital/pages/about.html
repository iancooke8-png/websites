About Us

Our Story

Family Capital was established in 1993. Over thirty-three years, we've built businesses, won 33 industry awards, and navigated every market condition imaginable — from fax machines to AI.

In that time, we've built and sold Benchmark Capital — a business that grew to nearly 300 staff before being acquired by Schroders. We've built four IFA businesses, two networks, and three technology platforms. We've seen every market cycle, every regulatory change, and every shift in how this industry works.

But one thing has remained the same throughout: we have never sold our client relationships. Every family we look after today, we've chosen to keep. That's thirty-three years of the same people, the same commitment, the same independence.

We became digital-first long before it was fashionable. We built our own technology — the Wealth Portal, our investment platform, our compliance systems — because independence means delivering the best possible service to our clients, on our terms. That thinking runs through everything we do.

We value our independence. We value our clients. And after thirty-three years, we're still here — still privately owned, still doing it our way.

Our first win — the Money Marketing Awards, 2011. 32 more followed.

About Us

Our Story

Founded in 1993, Family Capital has spent three decades at the forefront of wealth management for Ultra-High Net Worth individuals and entrepreneurs. In 2017 we rebranded, having built and sold Benchmark Capital to Schroders — a milestone that cemented our reputation for building exceptional financial businesses.

Today, we manage substantial wealth across a growing client base, combining deep financial planning expertise with cutting-edge technology. We are digital-first, technology-led, and relentlessly client-centred — because the families we serve deserve nothing less.

Our approach is simple: understand what matters most to each client, then build the structures, strategies, and relationships to protect and grow their wealth for generations.

Our Approach

The principles that guide everything we do

Digital-First

We believe technology should remove friction, not create it. Our proprietary platforms give clients and advisers real-time visibility, seamless reporting, and effortless access to their entire financial world.

Client-Centred

Every family is different. We take the time to understand the full picture — business interests, family dynamics, aspirations, and concerns — before crafting a strategy that truly fits.

Built to Last

We think in decades, not quarters. From trust structures and Family Investment Companies to succession planning and intergenerational wealth transfer, everything we build is designed to endure.

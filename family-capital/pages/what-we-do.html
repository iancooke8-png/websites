What We Do

We look after your entire financial life.

Most firms carve financial advice into departments — wealth planning here, investment management there, tax over there. Separate teams, separate systems, separate conversations. We don't work like that.

At Family Capital, it's all one thing. Your pension, your investments, your trust, your family investment company, your protection, your tax planning — it all connects. One team, one platform, one complete picture of everything you own, owe, and need.

We call it holistic financial planning, but really it's just common sense. You can't plan someone's retirement without understanding their tax position. You can't structure a trust without knowing the investment strategy. You can't manage investments without knowing what they're for. It's all one thing.

Life doesn't happen in straight lines. Neither does your money.

This is why we plan holistically — because every milestone affects everything else. We built the cashflow tools that model your entire financial future.

Essential Lifestyle Income

We built the technology that makes this possible.

The Wealth Portal gives you and your adviser the same view — nothing hidden, nothing siloed. Your pensions, investments, trusts, properties, companies — all in one place. You can see everything, anytime, from anywhere.

Your adviser isn't spending two hours in traffic to tell you something you could see on a screen. They're spending that time actually talking to you, planning with you, and making sure everything is working together.

We built this technology ourselves — not because we're a tech company, but because nothing else was good enough. Independence matters, and that means controlling the tools we use to look after your money.

Other firms sell you a product and move on. We sit beside you for decades.

We've looked after some of our families for over thirty years. We've seen their children grow up, their businesses succeed, their plans evolve. We don't do one-off transactions — we build relationships that last.

If that sounds like what you're looking for, we'd love to hear from you.

One event can unwind decades of planning.

Life cover, critical illness, key person insurance. Not exciting. But without it, a single event can destroy decades of careful work.

And we always put policies in trust — every single time. Why wouldn't you? Without the trust, the proceeds go through probate and potentially into the estate for IHT. With a trust, they go where you want them, when you want them.

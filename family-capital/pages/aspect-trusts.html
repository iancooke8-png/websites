Trusts

(move wealth between generations — properly)

Trusts, family investment companies, settlements. These are the structures that preserve wealth across generations — but only when integrated with everything else.

Trusts are the final step because they depend on everything before them.

You can't set up a trust without understanding the tax position. You can't fund it without understanding the investment strategy. You can't protect it without the right cover in place. That's why this is step eight — not because it's least important, but because it only works when everything else is in order.

Family Investment Companies are one of the most powerful planning tools available.

A FIC lets you gift future growth to your children while retaining control. You stay as a director. You decide what happens. But the shares — and therefore the future value — belong to the next generation. Properly structured, this can significantly reduce inheritance tax while keeping you in charge.

We've been setting these up for years. The structure, the articles of association, the share classes — it all matters. Get it wrong and you create problems, not solutions.

Simple trusts for straightforward situations.

Not everything needs a complex structure. A bare trust for a grandchild's investment, an absolute trust for a life policy — sometimes the simplest option is the right one. We always start with what you're trying to achieve, not with the most complicated structure we can find.

Flexibility comes at a cost — but sometimes it's worth it.

Discretionary trusts give trustees the power to decide who benefits and when. They're more complex, there are periodic and exit charges, and they require ongoing management. But for families where circumstances change — and they always do — the flexibility can be invaluable.

Setting up a trust is the easy part. Managing it is where the value lies.

Trusts need investment management, tax returns, trustee meetings, distributions, record-keeping. We build the technology that manages all of this — it's what our platform was designed for. Trustees get full visibility, full reporting, and full compliance support.

Already have trusts? We'll review them.

Many of our clients come to us with trusts already in place — sometimes set up years ago by a different adviser. We review the structure, the investments, the tax position, and whether the trust still achieves what it was designed to do. Sometimes it does. Sometimes it needs updating. Either way, we'll tell you.

How this connects to your wider plan

Trusts are the final piece — but they connect back to protection, taxation, investments, and the very first step: understanding what you owe and what you own.
